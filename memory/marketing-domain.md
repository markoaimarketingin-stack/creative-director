# Creative Director Engine - Marketing Domain Knowledge

This document serves as the strategic marketing playbook for the Creative Director Engine, specifying performance ad constraints, direct-response copy guidelines, visual composition standards, and short-form video retention structures.

---

## 📈 Platform Constraints & Specifications

| Platform | Primary Text Max Length | Headline Max Length | Description Max Length | Available Aspect Ratios | Native Environment |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Meta** (FB & IG) | 125 characters (Max 12 words) | 40 characters (Max 7 words) | 30 characters (Max 5 words) | `1:1` (Square), `4:5` (Vertical), `9:16` (Stories/Reels) | Interactive feeds & stories |

## 🪝 Core Hook Categories

Scroll-stopping hooks are classified into five major direct-response categories:

1. **Curiosity**: Creates a gap in knowledge, forcing the user to stop and read further.
   - *Example*: "The one setting startup founders always overlook on Google Ads..."
2. **Fear-Based**: Focuses on cost, missed opportunities, or critical risks of continuing the status quo.
   - *Example*: "Every day you run ads without this tool, you're burning 30% of your budget."
3. **Benefit-Driven**: Directly displays the desired outcome or core value proposition clearly and immediately.
   - *Example*: "Generate high-converting creative packages in under 3 minutes."
4. **Contrarian**: Refutes standard industry advice or exposes a common myth.
   - *Example*: "Stop writing longer ad copy. Here is why short copy is winning."
5. **Social-Proof**: Uses numbers, growth indices, or testimonials to establish credibility.
   - *Example*: "Why over 1,200 growth marketers are switching to this AI engine."

## 📐 The Zero Meta-Composition Rule

When generating visuals, the engine enforces a strict styling rule to prevent visual clutter and unnatural screenshots:

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Zero Meta-Composition Rule:**
> Do NOT describe device containers (laptops, monitors, smartphones, screens), hands holding devices, office workspaces, desks, or stock-photo humans looking at screens.
> **Instead, describe the product UI, physical container, or benefit outcome directly.**

### Guidelines for Visual Concepts:
- **For SaaS & Apps**: Describe the app UI/dashboard interface directly (e.g., "Minimalist dashboard showing blue charts, dark gray theme, clear metric columns"). This forces the generator to design the interface natively filling the entire frame.
- **For Physical Products**: Place the product in premium settings using professional lighting and studio background palettes (e.g., "Product package sitting on a solid ceramic platform with soft overhead spotlighting").
- **Composition Style**: Emulate high-end commercial product photography (like Apple or Stripe product assets): premium, minimalist, hero-centric, clean margins, and solid color contrast.

## 📊 Creative Scoring Rubric

To filter out low-performing ad creatives, each generated concept/copy bundle is scored out of 100 based on four criteria:

1. **Clarity**: Can a viewer understand what product is being advertised in under 1 second?
2. **Persuasion**: Does the text create desire, present a compelling transformation, or trigger emotional interest?
3. **CTA Alignment**: Does the call-to-action make sense given the objective? (e.g., A conversion objective should use active, high-intent CTAs like "Get Started" rather than passive ones).
4. **Platform Fit**: Does the copy and aspect ratio match the native guidelines of the target channel without being clipped?

## 📹 Viral Reel Retention Mechanics

For short-form organic video (Instagram Reels and TikTok), viral growth depends entirely on viewer watch-time retention. The engine optimizes video scripts and storyboard logic for:

### 1. Retention Curve Optimization
- **The 3-Second Hook**: The hook must engage the user immediately. The script writes visual actions (e.g., zoom-ins, screen flashes, sudden movements) in tandem with the verbal hook.
- **Dopamine / Interruption Spikes**: Scene cuts or visual pattern interrupts must occur every 1.5 to 3 seconds to reset attention.
- **Replay Triggers**: Inserting fast-looping text overlays or visual loops that encourage users to re-watch the reel to understand the message.

### 2. Shoot-Ready Components
Every script has structural elements:
- **Dialogue & On-Screen Captions**: Words spoken vs. words overlaid on screen (words on screen should be shorter and more punchy than spoken voiceovers).
- **Edit Rhythm**: Fast-paced vs. slow-build rhythm guides.
- **Save/Share Triggers**: Captions that prompt users to "Save this for later" or "Share this with a founder who needs it" which signals organic distribution algorithms.
- **Viral Probability Scopes**: Calculating viral potential based on hook originality, emotional trigger density, niche relevance, and audio trend match.
